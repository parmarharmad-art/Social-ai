# 30-Day Hinglish Faceless Book-Summary Reels

This is a separate content lane for @officialkiraankumar. It does not replace the existing AI-automation content library or modify the existing Instagram publishing workflow.

## Files
- `book_summaries.json`: 30 original Hinglish voiceover packages, hooks, captions, three faceless scene directions, CTAs and hashtags.
- `validate_book_summaries.py`: deterministic structure, uniqueness, attribution and faceless-scene checks.
- `.github/workflows/book-summary-reels.yml`: manual/changed-file QA workflow that uploads a content-plan artifact. It does not render videos and does not publish anything.

## Android-first production
1. Open the artifact's JSON on Android or copy a day's voiceover into your preferred editor.
2. Record or synthesize narration using a voice tool whose terms permit your intended use.
3. Assemble faceless footage, captions and voiceover in a mobile editor such as Instagram Edits.
4. Review spelling, pronunciation, visuals, audio rights and book attribution.
5. Publish manually only after approval, then log the post URL and metrics in your tracker.

## Editorial and rights safeguards
- These are original summaries/reflections, not official book summaries or quotations.
- Verify each book's idea against a copy you are authorised to read before publishing.
- Do not use audiobook clips, scanned pages, long passages, copied cover art or unlicensed music.
- A book title/author attribution does not itself grant rights to reproduce text, cover art or audio.
- AI-generated narration/footage should be reviewed for factual accuracy and licensing.
- No claim of guaranteed growth or performance is made.

## Current scope
This workflow validates and packages the content plan. It does not create finished MP4 files, connect to Instagram, schedule posts, or publish. The existing Instagram publishing workflow is intentionally left unchanged.
