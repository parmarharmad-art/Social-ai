Your first four steps

A roadmap you can actually follow.

Keep each stage small enough to complete and review. Don't promise results you haven't earned yet.

01

Choose one service

Pick a focused offer such as content repurposing, simple reporting, or workflow documentation.

02

Learn by doing

Follow one small tutorial and recreate the result yourself, noting what you can and cannot do yet.

03

Build a demo

Create two or three sample projects and explain the before, process, and deliverable.

04

Get feedback

Share relevant samples, ask specific questions, and refine your offer based on real responses.

Start with one useful project.

Choose a skill, make a sample, and use it to guide your next step.
