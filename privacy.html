Information processed

Names, dates of birth, optional mobile digits, spelling candidates, vehicle registration text and selected report preferences are processed by the application server to calculate a report. The app does not intentionally save report inputs to its own database. Hosting infrastructure may process technical logs.

Optional AI interpretation

If you actively select AI reflection and consent, report details are sent to the configured AI provider to generate an interpretation. Do not enter sensitive information.

Payments

If checkout is enabled, payment orders and payment verification are handled through Razorpay. The app sends the order amount, a hash binding the purchase to report inputs, and an optional receipt email to the payment provider. The app does not collect or store card details, UPI PINs, ATM PINs, passwords or OTPs. The payment provider may process information under its own privacy terms.

Retention and contact

The app does not currently provide customer accounts or report history. Add a verified contact method and specify any applicable retention, deletion, grievance and jurisdictional details before commercial launch.
